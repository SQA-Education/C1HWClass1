1. Reading

Improving Team Communication

A department is reviewing its meeting culture because discussions have become repetitive and poorly structured. If people arrive without a clear agenda, they tend to repeat points rather than move the discussion forward.

The team leader notices that when employees feel unsettled, they often remain silent instead of voicing concerns. To address the issue, the team drafts a new format for meetings.

If the staff submits their topics in advance, the facilitator will group them by theme. If the agenda is clear, participants will understand what decisions need to be made.
