This C1 homework consolidates Class 1 only: effective teamwork communication, active listening, workplace vocabulary, and advanced use of zero and first conditionals.

1. Reading

Improving Team Communication

A department is reviewing its meeting culture because employees have begun to feel that discussions are repetitive and poorly structured. The team leader notices that if people arrive without a clear agenda, they tend to repeat points rather than move the discussion forward. She also observes that when employees feel unsettled, they often remain silent instead of voicing their concerns.

To address the issue, the team drafts a new format. If staff members submit their topics in advance, the facilitator will collect them and group them by theme. If the team communicates the agenda clearly, participants will understand what decisions need to be made.

2. Vocabulary

Match the professional communication word to its best meaning.

Real-life English tip: At C1 level, words like address, voice, and showcase sound more precise than general verbs like talk about or show.

3. Writing
